Privacy Policy

Axiom Master for iPhone and iPad. Effective 21 November 2026.

Summary

Axiom Master does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your audio

All processing (analysis, mastering, encoding and export) happens on your device. Audio files you load, the reference tracks you compare against and the files you export are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. The app makes no network requests for its features.

Data stored on your device

To restore your last session, the app keeps your settings and a copy of the tracks you loaded in its own private storage on your device. Exported files are saved to the app's folder in the Files app. Deleting the app removes all of it. If you use iCloud or device backups, Apple may include this data in your backup under your own Apple Account.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is an in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app asks Apple only whether the purchase exists for your Apple Account. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
