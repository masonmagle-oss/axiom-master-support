Axiom Master

Audio mastering for iPhone and iPad. Everything runs on your device.

Getting help

Email support@lighttakesroot.com with questions, bug reports and feature requests. No account is needed. Please include your device, iOS version and what you were doing. The line under Tools > About in the app ("engine: ... meter: ...") is useful too.

If you have a GitHub account, you can also open an issue.

Quick answers

  • Which files can I load? WAV, FLAC, MP3, M4A/AAC and AIFF. Use Upload in the app, or "Open in Axiom Master" from Files or the share sheet. On iPad you can also drag files in.
  • Where do exports go? To the Axiom Master folder in the Files app, and the share sheet opens so you can send them anywhere.
  • What is free? All mastering tools, meters, the loudness report, and 16-bit WAV, MP3 and AAC export.
  • What does Pro add? A one-time purchase that unlocks 24-bit and 32-bit float WAV, 24-bit FLAC, embedded metadata and cover art, batch export, and the binaural, 7.1.2 and ADM BWF spatial exports. There is no subscription.
  • I bought Pro on another device. Open Tools > Pro and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Loudness targets

The platform targets in the app (for example -14 LUFS for Spotify) are the levels each service publishes for loudness normalisation. Services change these from time to time; check their own documentation before a release.
